    Commissions

    We take requests. Here is what we make and how it works. Every request is quoted individually.

    Services

    Custom nextbot / NPC (Garry's Mod)

    DrGBase nextbot from your image or model. Includes rig, walk/run/attack animations, sounds, ragdoll and spawn menu entry. quoted per request

    Character pack

    Several characters with a shared theme, one addon, one Workshop page. quote per pack

    Port to s&box

    Your character on SBHBase for s&box, with possession, factions and effects. quoted per request

    Player model / ragdoll

    Rig to the ValveBiped skeleton, compile for GMod, c_hands and bodygroups on request. quoted per request

    2D nextbot

    Classic PNG nextbot with your image and sounds. Also as a batch. quoted per request

    Tools and scripts

    Sandbox tools, ranking systems, minigames for NPCs. quote per project

    Every request is quoted individually

    Price and delivery date depend on the character (2D image vs finished 3D model), the number of animations, any special mechanics and our current workload. We do not publish fixed prices; you always get the exact number and date before we start, and nothing starts until you agree.

    How it works

    1. Tell us what you want. Image or 3D model, name, what it does (walk, run, attack, special sounds or effects). If it is based on an existing character, say so.
    2. We quote. We look at the request and reply with a price and a delivery date. Nothing starts until you agree.
    3. We build it. Modelling or import, rig, animation, sounds, compile, testing in game.
    4. You review a video. You see the character working in Garry's Mod (or s&box) before paying.
    5. Payment and delivery. You receive the addon folder (or Workshop upload if you prefer). Small fixes after delivery are included.

    Good to know

    • Turnaround. Depends on the character and on how many requests are in progress. The date we give you in the quote is the one we commit to.
    • Non-exclusive by default. Standard commissions may be added to our public packs after a short exclusivity window (2 days). If you need the character to stay private, ask for an exclusive licence when you request the quote.
    • Your IP is yours. We do not resell characters you own the rights to without your permission.
    • What we need from you. The clearest reference you have. A 3D model (FBX, Blend, OBJ) saves time and money; a good image is enough for a 2D nextbot or a simple 3D one.
    • Sound. Send your own sound files, or tell us the style and we source or record them.

    How to send a request

    There are two ways in, and only these two. Requests sent anywhere else can get lost, so we do not take them by YouTube comment, Steam message or Gmodstore.
